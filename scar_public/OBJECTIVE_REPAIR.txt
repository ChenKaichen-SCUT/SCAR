Requirement-bound trial verification and revision

Entry point: ObjectiveRepairAgent, opt-in arm "objective". It replaces the
hypothesis selector's finalize_decision hook, while reusing the ordinary actor,
public notebook, and existing action/resource checks. It is not a default
promotion or a new whole-game guarantee.

Decision loop
1. Before asking for a repair, compute a finite requirement catalog from the
   public task/recipe fragment and actually failed entrances. The LLM chooses
   one catalog identifier and a short method; it cannot choose a different
   target/count as that method's success criterion.
2. Compile the final test independently of step milestones. A source search
   checks the required material at the proposed location; acquisition checks
   material in hand; an access method checks the attempted destination.
   Access/acquisition/source programs must structurally reach their claimed
   endpoint under the existing partial projection. Unknown effects can still
   be tested. Matching a dialogue report does not fulfill the task requirement.
3. Execute one action and read the actual observation. Record separately:
   execution milestone, proposed condition, and requirement fulfillment.
   A visible adequate source may select a checked pickup. Finding a source
   and acquiring its material are separate outcomes.
4. A false or unknown trial schedules one bounded revision event for the SAME
   unmet requirement. Provide the actual observation, failed plan and scoped
   evidence. Do not wait for another repetition trigger. Urgent actions yield
   first; a changed task cancels the old requirement.
5. A recently observed inadequate source rejects the same source proposal even
   if its wording/route changes. This is local evidence, not global impossibility.
   Changed target quantity, sufficient held material, task change or 24-action
   expiry revokes it. Unknown visibility is not absence; only an unchanged
   experiment in the same represented state is deferred.
6. Missing structured hypotheses and rejected plans receive at most one
   correction. Ordinary proposals use at most 10 of the 12 shared calls; two
   remain available for feedback-driven revision. If no admissible new plan
   is generated, preserve the ordinary actor instead of inventing a remedy.

Scope and limitations
The catalog is an incomplete computable fragment of public task descriptions,
not a verified semantic parse of arbitrary goals. Recipe inputs are method
requirements, not simultaneous terminal obligations. Failed entrances define
attempted subproblems, not globally necessary destinations: an alternative
that bypasses the destination may be missed. Dialogue branch relevance remains
heuristic. The gate checks represented conditions; quotes do not prove causal
relations, and the checks do not prove eventual task benefit or survival.

Negative-evidence reuse is revocable and heuristic in a changing world. Full
observation of the listed ground inventory is assumed for source absence;
unobserved replenishment before expiry can make that evidence stale.
Ordinary actor actions outside triggered repair trials (including destructive
craft/disassembly actions) are not globally supervised by this mechanism.
Only pickup/navigation/inspection/dialogue programs are represented. No new
entity-specific resource rule or irreversible-action veto is introduced.

Tests cover renamed entities, false versus unknown observations, quantities,
capacity, immediate revision, source-specific rejection and revocation,
unrelated endpoints, schema correction, task/urgent-action preservation and
the shared query budget. See experiments/objective_protocol.txt and
results/2026-10-18-objective-revision/RESULTS.txt for measured behavior and limits.
