SCAR: competing resource semantics and opportunity-preserving experiments
Implementation date: 2026-10-16. This is a research prototype, not a novelty claim.

1. EXACT SCOPE

The generic finite domain supports multiple recipes, finite observed locations,
directed routes, hand/bag/floor resources, capacity constraints, protected resource
totals, craft events and observation-contingent policies. The automatic V6 adapter
currently grounds ONE observed recipe at the CURRENT observed station. It does
not automatically ground upstream recipes or remote material acquisition. Those
capabilities are tested in the generic domain, not established end to end in TTCL.

The local goal is newly craft AND hold the chosen output. Ingredients are consumed
preconditions, not goal conjuncts. The LLM/V6 chooses this METHOD milestone. It is
not a replacement for the natural-language quest or proof of the quest translation.
Default protected_totals is empty: unrelated future quests are not certified.

2. FINITE COMPETING MODELS

One unit is 1000 exact integer milli-units. For an ingredient coefficient c and
integer batch q, candidates predict:
  exact:           d(c,q) = c*q
  ceil_batch:      d(c,q) = 1000*ceil(c*q/1000)
  ceil_per_output: d(c,q) = 1000*q*ceil(c/1000)

Accessibility alternatives are hand; hand+attached bag; hand+current floor;
hand+attached bag+current floor. Sufficiency checks SUM the accessible quantities
of EACH ingredient before any consumption. Fixed consumption priority is hand,
bag, floor. This priority, fixed item loads, failure-without-consumption, output
on floor, station sufficiency and absent ambient changes are DECLARED ASSUMPTIONS.
They are not inferred truths and need not cover the real game. Independent recipes
have independent parameter choices. Equivalent profiles are collapsed with aliases
recorded: for example the two ceiling rules cannot be distinguished with q=1 only.

State x consists of location, elapsed actions, craft-event bits, and all finite
resource counts by store. Every action has cost 1, including failed craft and
inspection. Preconditions/effects are explicit in ResourceDomain.transition.
K(x) checks nonnegative counts, hand/bag capacities, and protected total minima.
Goal G(x) checks the craft event and current output possession. Bag contents are
hidden except during inspect BAG. Remote floor counts are not observations. Model
IDs and success-exemption labels are NEVER observation fields.

B is a set of pairs (m,x), all initially compatible with the grounded public prefix.
For action a and observed o:
  B[a,o] = {(m,T_m(x,a)) : (m,x) in B, O_m(x,a)=o}.
Empty actual successor belief is MODEL_MISMATCH, not success or proof of no solution.

3. TWO DIFFERENT GUARANTEES

STRONG mode requires a single observable policy that reaches G in EVERY initial
candidate world while satisfying K throughout. For example, a hand-only recipe
requiring 3 items with capacity 2 makes this guarantee impossible.

PRESERVE_OPPORTUNITY mode fixes the ORIGINAL belief B0 and horizon H0. Solve each
world individually under K and H0. A world may be success-exempt only when its
individual optimum is PROVED infinite by exact search or a validated infeasibility
certificate. Budget exhaustion is UNKNOWN and NEVER earns an exemption. Let E0 be
these proved-infeasible worlds and R0=B0\E0. E0 is frozen for the entire trial.
It is never recomputed after consuming time or resources. Every model, including
exempt models, stays in the belief and in every K check. If R0 is empty, no trial
is selected: a vacuous success guarantee is not a reason to experiment.

For every world in R0 a single observation-based policy must reach G by H0;
for every world in B0 it must obey K. A branch containing only E0 may stop without
G, explicitly reported as REMAINING_MODELS_ORIGINALLY_INFEASIBLE. This is NOT task
completion, nor evidence those models are false. Infeasibility is relative to the
bounded grounded fragment: it says nothing about unmodeled routes, other methods,
larger budgets or the full game. Consequently this guarantee must not be described
as unconditional real-world task safety or guaranteed quest completion.

4. ADMISSIBILITY AND LOWER BOUND

A finite prefix is informative when its public observation partitions the live
models into at least two nonempty proper subsets. The implemented prefix search
only considers nonbranching preparations up to the FIRST split. Each resulting
branch must have ONE common observable continuation satisfying the frozen success
obligations and all K constraints, within H0 minus every action already spent.
The quantifiers are: for each OBSERVATION BRANCH, there exists one POLICY that works
for all required WORLDS in that branch. Separate omniscient plans per world do not
certify an experiment.

Let L_m(x,h) be the existing certified LP relaxation for a goal-required world.
For a current belief B and remaining horizon h:
  L(B,h) = max {L_m(x,h) : (m,x) in B and m not in E0}, max(empty)=0.
Any K violation is infeasible. If all non-exempt worlds already satisfy G, stop is
allowed. Exempt worlds remain in all successor branches and constraint checks.

Proof of the lower bound. A shared feasible policy induces a concrete goal-reaching
trajectory for every required world. Its transition-count vector satisfies the
existing LP resource/symbol-flow relaxation by telescoping actual changes; relaxing
preconditions/order/integrality cannot raise the minimum above this trajectory's
cost. Only exact-rational-validated dual/Farkas certificates supply nonzero lower
bounds or infeasibility; floating primal values never prune. Hence L_m is at most
that world's trajectory cost, so the maximum is at most the policy's worst cost.
Dropping exempt-world goal bounds preserves this inequality. K may still invalidate
an exempt-world trajectory. All needed reachable transitions up to H0 are compiled;
if grounding exceeds its finite-state bound, compilation aborts with UNKNOWN.

Proposition (conditional opportunity preservation). Assume the true world is in
the fixed candidate family, all transition/observation/load/frame assumptions hold,
and the original world has a K-respecting solution to the local goal within H0.
If the selector approves a prefix and every branch continuation is replay-verified,
executing the prefix and the continuation indexed by real observations preserves
and realizes that local completion opportunity within H0.

Proof. A genuinely initially feasible modeled world cannot be in E0, because only
sound infeasibility proofs grant exemption. At each step its actual successor and
observation occur in the predicted branch by model correctness. Induction keeps
this world in the live belief. Prefix legality gives K before the split, and the
verified branch policy gives K afterward and G at its stop for every required world,
including this one. The explicit remaining horizon counts all prefix/continuation
actions. Frozen E0 prevents a newly damaged feasible world being relabeled exempt.
Composition proves the result. If observations reject every model, the assumptions
have failed; the executor invalidates the policy and makes no guarantee.

This is a conditional correctness statement for this implementation, not a theorem
that LLM-generated world models cover reality, that translation is correct, that
every safe experiment can be found, or that the procedure is a novel planning result.

5. SEARCH PSEUDOCODE

compile every reachable candidate transition through H0, else return UNKNOWN
E0 := empty in strong mode; otherwise only proved initially infeasible worlds
baseline := bounded AND/OR solve(B0,H0,E0)
for bounded nonbranching prefix ending at its first public split:
    reject if any intermediate world violates K
    for every observation branch B_o:
        continuation := bounded AND/OR solve(B_o,H0-prefix_cost,E0)
        reject unless a finite policy is found and replayed in ALL worlds of B_o
    total := prefix_cost + maximum verified continuation cost
    reject if baseline has a verified policy and total exceeds its cost + allowance
choose admissible prefix by (worst total cost, largest remaining model set,
    excess target production, prefix length, deterministic lexical tie break)
if none, use baseline verified direct policy if present; otherwise abstain
execute one action; read fresh public feedback; retain only matching successors
if missing observation, unexpected equipment/load/combat or zero survivors: invalidate
otherwise follow the common policy's observable branch and repeat

The cost allowance defaults to ZERO. It compares with a known verified baseline
upper bound, not necessarily the optimal cost; bounded search cannot prove that
all cheaper policies or all better experiments were considered. The ranking uses
no invented probabilities or entropy prior. Information is secondary to feasibility
and cost. An unnecessary inspection or redundant production can lose to direct work.

6. IMPLEMENTATION AND VALIDATION

resource_competition.py: quantity/access domain, finite compiler, opportunity mode,
selector; reuses unmodified finite_planner.py and its certified LP/search.
resource_trial.py: public grounding, strict fresh observation matching, branch execution.
resource_agent.py: opt-in resource_competition_v8 on V6, target from V6/LLM; ongoing
certified continuations need no extra LLM request. Public feedback and the initial
problem persist; resume reconstructs/verifies the same policy. Six grounded local
search attempts per run (missing-evidence admission failures do not spend this
budget, corrected 2026-10-17), six-action horizon, 300-action global ceiling, no extra probe
cost allowance. Gaps return to V6 with an audit, without a whole-agent safety claim.
diagnose_resources.py: fixed public prefixes, optional isolated checkpoint execution.

Diagnostics used pre-action public prefixes at mark/36, metropolis/86, remnant/174.
Planning never received the stored future action/outcome, hidden engine state or
reward. Environments were restored only afterward by a separate evaluator.
mark: craft 1 oak_rod reduced 4 effective models to 2 (exact 0.5 rejected), then
pick up oak_rod confirmed the local milestone. Access alternatives remain unresolved.
metropolis: craft 1 gavel_club reduced 4 models to 2 (bag-inaccessible predictions
rejected), then pickup confirmed the milestone; the environment also emitted a
quest-stage completion message. Floor accessibility and resource priority are NOT
identified by this success. The initially impossible hand-only model remained in
the failure branch during certification; it was eliminated only by actual success.
remnant: the floor-accessible world needs six actions even when its model is known.
A failed craft probe would leave five, so its previously feasible opportunity is
lost. This informative probe is rejected; no environment action was executed there.
Two selected local trials, four actual environment actions, zero API calls. These
are selected checkpoint diagnostics, not independent benchmark runs or evidence of
improved 300-step total scores. No frozen baseline was rerun.

Tests include rejecting a resource-destroying informative batch, hidden bag contents
requiring a paid observation, declining unnecessary probing, fixed exemptions,
UNKNOWN retention, independent minimax comparisons, upstream/cross-location generic
planning, real-feedback rejection, resumed policy reconstruction and V6 integration.

Next empirical question: after broadening automatic grounding with validated upstream
effects, compare the active controller against V6 on held-out full runs under the
same 300-step budget. Do not infer that result from these two local successes.
