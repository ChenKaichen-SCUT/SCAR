Specification before action: research hypothesis and literature boundaries

Problem. A verified source excerpt does not establish the inferred goal semantics. A valid
action does not establish a feasible joint arrangement. A feasible plan can still satisfy a
mis-specified goal. These are separate obligations, not interchangeable success indicators.

Maintain proposed goal interpretations G, a partial observation-derived transition/constraint
model M, and public evidence E. Evaluate each candidate predicate in {true,false,unknown}.
Finding a plan pi with M predicting G at its end establishes only model-relative feasibility.
Do not infer actual task success until matching public completion feedback is observed.

Specification occurs before action generation and cannot be rewritten by the action response.
This removes one direct path for the actor to invent a convenient success test. It does NOT
guarantee semantic faithfulness: the separate specification request can still mistranslate.

For numeric arrangements, enforce known constraints at every intermediate state: count of
held units <= observed capacity; material counts cannot be duplicated by moves; all required
inputs must be available together at the operation; a known station must be at the location.
Only current observations establish current state. Missing fields are unknown, not zero.
The present solver handles local inventory rearrangement, not global numeric task planning.
Unverified storage/crafting semantics remain explicit assumptions tested by execution.

For ambiguity, an experiment q is potentially distinguishing only when two hypotheses predict
different values of one observable Y under q. Comparing paraphrased predictions is insufficient.
If Y is unavailable, retain both hypotheses. If preconditions are incomplete or effects are
stochastic, mismatches are conditional evidence, not sound elimination. Non-destructive probes
should be preferred, but the prototype does not assume that cheap actions are reversible.

Verified related work (primary sources, accessed 2026-10-15)

LLM+P (2023), https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11477
Translates natural-language planning descriptions to PDDL and delegates search to a classical
planner. Specification followed by symbolic planning is established prior work.

CaStL (2024), https://arxiv.org/html/2410.22225v1
Extracts goal, ordering and blocking constraints through language translation, using PDDL/Python
and a solver. Goal/constraint extraction alone cannot be claimed as our novelty.

LLM-Modulo evaluation (2024), https://arxiv.org/html/2411.14484v1
Uses candidate generation and external critics. Its correctness statements depend on sound
critics; the reported scheduling domains provide all needed specification information. Our
partially observed text adapter and learned assumptions do not inherit such guarantees.

Asking the Right Questions / Agent Interrogation (AAAI 2021),
https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.12613
Learns interpretable action models via queries designed to distinguish candidates. Distinguishing
experiments and model elimination are established ideas. Our real trajectory cannot freely
reset or query arbitrary hypothetical states online, and carries missing-observation/confounding
risks not removed by simply borrowing the phrase "active experiment."

RAMP / numeric action-model learning (2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13006
Combines execution data, numeric action-model learning, planning and reinforcement learning in
Minecraft. Learning numeric constraints while acting is also prior work; V5 is much narrower.

From Solver Feedback to Faithful Plans (2026 preprint),
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21897v1
Explicitly distinguishes solvability of generated PDDL from fidelity to the original task.
Its multi-role reinforcement learning differs from this inference-only prototype, but the
underlying problem of solver success rewarding a changed goal is directly related.

Potential research contribution, not established novelty:
Jointly track uncertainty in the TASK INTERPRETATION and the ACTION MODEL; use the earliest
unresolved obligation to decide between executing, repairing a numeric arrangement, and gathering
discriminating evidence. Test in persistent, partially observed environments without hidden
state or model resets. To make this a paper contribution requires a precise update/selection
rule and evidence that it helps beyond specification-only, solver-only and matched-compute
LLM feedback baselines. The current implementation is an exploratory approximation.

Required reporting: actual task reward, local repair rate, premature success claims, unresolved
predicates, conditional-model prediction errors, experiment outcomes, API tokens/cost and total
environment steps. A lower invalid-action count without task gain is insufficient. Reusing known
failure checkpoints establishes local development evidence, not unseen-task generalization.
