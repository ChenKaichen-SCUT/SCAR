Important negative case
mark_r1 drops from 17/passed under v2 to 5/not passed, exactly the checked
trajectory. The removed question repeated the existing copper-sword instruction;
it did not identify a new source. The old trajectory later picked up a key at
decision 59 and entered the armory at 60. A single divergent continuation cannot
show that the redundant dialogue caused the eventual success through information.
Nevertheless the regression is real and cannot be discarded as irrelevant.

Scope and limits
The downstream check covers the represented navigation prefix and alternate
source route, not a full natural-language quest or complete upstream crafting
plan. Failure reuse is conditional on task, information revision and carried
resources and expires after six actions (including projected travel). It is
a conservative heuristic deferral with omitted conditions, not a safety theorem.
The actor can retain its own original action. We do not permanently ban edges.
Information branches currently need two publicly observed sources and concrete
subsequent actions. That strict coverage requirement can remove useful exploration.
The remaining bottleneck is constructing a justified route that changes the
blocking condition; filtering an unhelpful route does not construct such a route.
No new independent worlds or repetitions were used; do not claim significance.
The candidate schema/prompt and continuation handling changed together. Full-game
scores compare versions, not an isolated identical-proposal authority ablation.
