Primary-source check, 2026-10-17

The problem is not an untouched research gap. The following mechanisms can
address parts of it; none of the cited results establishes success on our exact
saved game states without running that method there.

ReflAct: World-Grounded Decision Making in LLM Agents via Goal-State Reflection
https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15182
EMNLP 2025. Continuously relates the current state to the task goal to improve
grounding and action selection. This is a relevant goal-directed reflection
baseline; our claim cannot be simply that other agents never consider goals.

AdaPlanner: Adaptive Planning from Feedback with Language Models
https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16653
Uses in-plan and out-of-plan refinement driven by environment feedback, a code
style planning prompt and successful plans as reusable exemplars. Feedback-led
plan revision is established prior work, not an independent SCAR contribution.

Sentry: Learning to Recover from LLM Agent Failures at Test Time
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02994v1
Section 3 detects failure, retrieves matching recovery lessons, supplies soft
repair guidance and verifies subsequent recovery before storing a lesson.
Its 200 paired intervention forks report 74 higher final scores, 101 ties and
25 lower scores. These are that paper's benchmarks and verifier, not our games.
This is evidence that conditional failure guidance can improve final outcomes;
an explicit executable gate still has to earn its added cost in our experiments.

Language Agent Tree Search Unifies Reasoning, Acting, and Planning in Language Models
https://arxiv.org/html/2310.04406v3
Uses tree selection, expansion, evaluation, simulation, outcome backpropagation
and reflection. Downstream outcomes therefore affect earlier choices. The paper
explicitly discusses needing an environment model or undo/reset capability.
Our online actor has no private environment replay; evaluator replay must not
be silently granted to a planning baseline without accounting for that change.

CPR: Regression-based Planning with Sensing Actions and Incomplete Information
https://www.cs.nmsu.edu/~tson/CPR/
Author's planner page. Uses state-based regression under a stated approximate
semantics to generate conditional/conformant plans from a formal problem with
fluents, operators, initial knowledge and goals. Outcome-conditioned plans and
reasoning backward from required conditions are established planning ideas.
Applying them to incomplete public language evidence adds a modeling problem;
calling the two ideas themselves new would overstate our contribution.

Research implication (our assessment)
The potentially useful distinction is a cheap, revocable check on candidate
plans and concrete outcome-dependent continuations built from public evidence.
That distinction is an implementation hypothesis, not demonstrated scientific
superiority. The current development evidence must be reported together with
lost coverage, regressions, and the absence of qualified real information trials.
