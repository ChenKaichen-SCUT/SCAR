V12: task-anchored method review and preservation of material options
17 October 2026. Development results, not a held-out or repeated evaluation.

Implemented
- A method change retains an exact pending task quotation. Recipe existence or
  suggestive names do not establish the output's task role. Named task literals,
  explicit public category descriptions and numeric dependency paths support
  necessary roles only. Actual task completion remains a public feedback event.
- Audit consumption under all three supported quantity rules. Preserve a
  disjunction of candidate material routes rather than summing ingredients for
  all alternative methods. For supported substitutions, check the selected
  endpoint and other explicitly parsed commitments on one shared material ledger.
  Materials reserved for the old method can be released by an evidenced same-task
  alternative. Unknown recipes, stale contents and missing prerequisites do not
  become a certificate.
- Unsupported resource-consuming substitutes are blocked before execution. A
  bounded repair can restore a previously observed inventory capability and then
  return to the original dependency target. Every inspection, navigation, storage
  and craft action is charged. Navigation/survival and arbitrary goal semantics
  are not certified by the material relaxation.
- An important semantic correction: gavel_club cannot be equipped by the equip
  command either. Its pickup DOES produce the weapon-task completion report.
  Thus the old splint diagnosis cannot rely solely on a failed equip command.

What the theory now establishes
1. Under faithful finite effect profiles and observed stock, the componentwise
   option check retains at least one stocked candidate route, in O(M R D) time.
   This is material availability, not capacity/accessibility/terminal feasibility.
2. Shared-ledger recursive production yields a constructive joint material
   witness when it succeeds. Failure/timeout/cycles return unknown, not impossibility.
3. Full task-preserving substitution additionally requires a valid task bridge
   and one verified observation-based continuation per observation leaf within
   the remaining action budget. V12's general natural-language repair does not
   supply all these premises. The working supplement separates this conditional
   theorem from the implemented necessary checks.

Remaining performance gap
The current trigger occurs after a grounded failed craft and is too narrow.
The next substantive improvement must keep the original task binding at method
selection time, distinguish legitimate upstream steps from goal substitution,
and obtain naturally occurring interventions before claiming a game-score gain.
The current result supports a cheap, useful selected repair mechanism; it does
not support declaring the full-game performance problem solved.
