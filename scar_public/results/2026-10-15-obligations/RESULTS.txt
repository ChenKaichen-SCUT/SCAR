Changes:
1. Preserve V3's chronological evidence. Remove model-generated terminal-condition lists.
   A narrow grammar produces only source-grounded explicit named crafted/held/equipped
   checks. Ingredients are method dependencies. Unbound categories and other semantics
   remain unknown; supported predicates are not a sufficient task-completion certificate.
2. Search known recipe dependencies and routes with joint counts, capacity and a carried
   container. Execute one action and observe. Current adjacency and recent failed traversal
   invalidate stale route proposals. Contradicted ingredient arrangements feed back into the
   model; repeated counterexamples suspend automatic recipe use, not all actor tests.
3. Pending public writing obligations with an explicit role-bound literal and observed
   operands can activate inspection/intervention/inspection without an actor request.
   Compare append vs replace only on a freshly inspected nonempty document, where the
   predicted full strings differ. Reuse existing operation evidence rather than count a
   redundant experiment. Goal-binding alternatives are tracked separately and conditionally.

Limits: narrow literal-role grammar and writing-specific active experiments; a partial observed

Originality is not established: language-to-specification, symbolic planning, active queries
and goal/world hypotheses already exist (see SPECIFICATION_RESEARCH.txt). The general research
question concerns when a contradicted model should trigger execution, repair, or a costly probe;
this prototype does not establish a general solution. Formal work needs held-out tasks,
matched-compute ablations and repeated runs under the intended official benchmark settings.
