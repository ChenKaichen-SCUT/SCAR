Selected 30-action diagnostics did show local gains: cp62 8->10 and revised cp206 10->12;

Controls were reused, not rerun; environment budget is matched but inference budget is not.

Implemented: independent proposed task specification; public observation adapter; three-valued
condition evaluation; bounded local inventory search; one-step execution and feedback; an
optional interface for discriminating semantic experiments. Online policy receives no hidden
state or reward decomposition. No game-specific recipes, phrases, capacities or solutions.

Full-run mechanism counts: 47 direct numeric-arrangement actions; 0 registered semantic
experiments. The semantic branch is not demonstrated. A field/schema is not a working policy.

Observed counterexamples: mark step138 adds held(copper_bar) to the copper-sword terminal goal,
mixing means with ends. Metropolis repeatedly uses the category 'weapon' as a concrete object
or 'equipped' as a public report value. Such expressions are parseable but not grounded goal
semantics. Local inventory search does not solve missing upstream recipes, distant resources,
navigation under changed conditions, or combat. De-duplicated source lines omit action order
and recurrence; the actor is not given the public action-transition ledger. These are plausible
mechanisms supported by traces/code, not isolated causal attribution of the complete regression.

Interpretation: establish proposed observable task semantics before committing to a method,
but preserve uncertainty when public evidence cannot identify a unique goal. Separate goal
fidelity, joint feasibility under a partial model, and actual observed completion. A stronger
next design would make unresolved goal/model obligations control action selection, rather
than adding another reflection field. Numeric contradictions need supported assumptions;
semantic trials need distinct predictions for the same observable with established conditions.

Novelty is unestablished. LLM+P, CaStL, LLM-Modulo, Agent Interrogation and RAMP already cover
major ingredients. Tru-POMDP already maintains joint world/goal hypotheses, and Bayesian
Active Learning for Intent Disambiguation selects informative task-specification questions.
The potential research question is goal-interpretation/action-model
uncertainty jointly guiding costly online queries in persistent partially observed worlds.
Require a precise selection/update rule, matched-compute ablations and held-out tasks before
claiming a contribution. Single development rollouts are not statistical evidence. No further
paid candidate tuning in this phase. See SPECIFICATION_RESEARCH.txt for primary-source links.
