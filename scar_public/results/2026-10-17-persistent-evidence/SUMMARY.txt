GUARDED PERSISTENT EVIDENCE: MECHANISM AND LIMITS (2026-10-17)

Implemented persistent_resource_v9 as an opt-in extension of V8. Matched public
transitions update a context/epoch ledger of raw JOINT quantity/access profiles.
Subsequent queries filter these profiles before compiling/searching. Compiled
model IDs are never reused as identities. All behavioral aliases are preserved;
unit-batch evidence cannot silently decide a larger-batch rounding rule.
Notebook checkpoints store the ledger and each active trial's frozen filter.
Mismatch or incomplete feedback revokes the epoch and cancels continuation.

Guard matching checks recipe/station evidence, location/routes, item vocabulary,
equipment/bag attachment, capacities/background loads, declared assumptions,
semantic version and universe. Counts and offered batches may change because
the next state and its policy are rebuilt. Guard adequacy and within-guard
stationarity remain assumptions; hidden time/level effects are not established.

No LLM call was made for this audit. No full game or unchanged baseline was rerun.
The evidence intervention adds no prompt section. Initial feedback text is the
same; future prompts can naturally differ once plans/actions/history differ.

CONTROLLED DECISION COMPARISON

Two constructed instances: ore only in an attached bag, hand capacity one or two.
A synthetic, explicitly modeled craft observation identifies bag accessibility.
At the later matched query (with replenished bag contents), the original family
needs take-out/craft/pick-up: three actions. The filtered family needs craft/pick-up:
two actions. Thus the rule affects an actual planned action, not only model count.
The acquisition experiment has worst cost four versus a three-action reference;
it uses allowance one. The full-game configuration still uses allowance zero.
This is NOT a demonstrated amortized saving across all candidate worlds.
When ore is already held, both families require two actions: information alone
does not imply a benefit. This no-benefit control is covered by the tests.

CHRONOLOGICAL PUBLIC-GAME AUDIT

Input: all 11 grounded local queries in results/2026-10-17-llm-bridge/results.json.
For every query, use only earlier actual public action feedback in that game.
Historical execution is replayed under the historical unfiltered policy. Filtered
policies are offline queries; historical outcomes are never assigned to them.

Queries: 11. Queries reusing a prior exclusion under matching guard: 0.
Changed decisions: 0. New game-score improvement established: NONE.

Repeated gavel queries before learning have no evidence yet. After the equipped
bag trial, later gavel queries have no attached bag, so the guard differs.
The oak-rod evidence is conservatively revoked after ambient combat; the
copper-sword bonus rejects the entire modeled family. Other learned recipes do
not recur in the grounded query list. These are applicability/coverage limits,
not a reason to relax guards merely to manufacture a positive replay result.

audit.json includes full ledger provenance and per-query compilation/search times.
Timings are one local diagnostic measurement, not an independent scale benchmark.
