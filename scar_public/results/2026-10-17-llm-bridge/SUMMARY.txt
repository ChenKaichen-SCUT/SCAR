THEORY-EXPERIMENT BOUNDARY

This treatment uses the earlier finite resource-semantic planner and certified
LP relaxation. It does NOT call sparse_policy.py, symbolic_coverage.py, or the
later candidate-coverage relation routines. Hence none of this score gain is
evidence of the later coverage bound's independent practical contribution.

The coverage theory proves sound rejection under its fixed finite task model,
specified separations from defined weaker controls, and preservation of exact
search guarantees. It does not prove correct natural-language goal extraction,
coverage of actual game dynamics, frequent extra pruning, or net runtime gain.
Its fixed nonnegative resource-consumption assumptions do not directly describe
the automatic crafting adapter with production and unknown effects.

Decision: pursue a controlled LLM-game hypothesis test, but do not present this
as a completed theory-backed game paper. Next isolate planning from prompt/model
feedback on matched public states. To connect the new coverage theory, first
build a validated encoding of real game-derived subproblems; compare scalar and
relational pruning under identical models, goals, budgets and search. Only an
additional verified rejection or net search benefit can support that claim.
If the benefit is instead from model revision or goal control, center the paper
on that mechanism and retain only theory that actually supports it.
