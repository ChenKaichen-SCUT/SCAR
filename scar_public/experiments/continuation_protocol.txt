Continuation repair development protocol, 2026-10-17

Primary objective going forward: task score under a 300-action episode budget.
This development experiment does not rewrite the historical frozen protocol.

Scope: replay the 9 historical checked trajectories with prefix-only evidence;
choose the first trigger in each episode, irrespective of subsequent outcome.
There are 6 eligible episodes (Mark and Metropolis); Remnant has no trigger.
For each eligible episode, fork the exact pre-action state for 40 actions, never
past episode step 300. Compare original actor (off), the same repair evidence and
LLM proposal with actor authority (advice), and checked candidate choice (repair).
Prior actor/API decoding, task memory and resource planner are shared unchanged.
Source is frozen before branch calls. Reuse exact historical baseline responses;
an uncached baseline request is an error, not permission to rerun the baseline.
Use recorded hash seed and complete environment/RNG snapshots for pairing.

The actor sees public text and its own executed feedback only. Scores, hidden
snapshots and future historical actions are evaluator-only. Report every branch,
including negative and unchanged outcomes. No choosing best branch or retry.
Diagnostics: action differences, valid interface/access checks, further explicit
failures, task-score change, deaths, and paid usage. No immediate-action success
is interpreted as full-policy success. These states already participated in
development; neither branch gains nor same-family seeds establish generalization.

Preservation: no trigger => identical actor prompts/action/frame/state; changing
resource or information context, expiry, unseen state or urgent threats releases
deferral. Repetition during productive operations is not by itself a trigger.
Admitted repair actions are observed movement/dialogue/inspection interfaces;
no deliberate inventory consumption is automatically substituted. These actions
still spend time and can encounter danger. Quotes prove provenance, not meaning.

Known limitations: thresholds 6/24 and candidate relevance are heuristics;
unobserved changes may invalidate an apparent repetition. This mechanism has no
guarantee of preserving every correct action, improving score, or solving combat.
Current first-step checks do not certify an entire continuation plan.
