SCAR local requirement-plan repair (experimental opt-in arm)

Purpose
When a trial fails, keep the requirement it was meant to satisfy and repair
its method. A different command, a successful move, and a completed material
requirement are different outcomes. This implementation makes that distinction
executable; performance improvement must still be established experimentally.

Entry points
scar_public.plan_repair.PlanRepairAgent
scar_public.experiments.core_trial.build_trial_agent('plan', ...)
scar_public.experiments.obstacle_trial --agent plan --steps 300
The ordinary actor and old default arms remain available. The experimental
arm does not stack another paid selector after the old repair selector.

Decision loop
1. Read actual public observation into the existing public model and notebook.
2. If a compiled action was pending, check its actual outcome. Retain observed
   milestones, but resolve the root only with its own condition: material in
   hand at the required quantity, or actual arrival for an access subproblem.
3. Continue a bounded active plan if the next prerequisite has a supported
   executable action. Yield to the ordinary actor's urgent actions.
4. If a prerequisite is unresolved, ask about that same child requirement.
   The LLM proposes at most two methods using supplied evidence and site IDs:
   source, known recipe, access hypothesis, or container inspection/retrieval.
   IDs establish provenance, not that a causal hypothesis is correct.
5. Compile the next action from public directed routes, observed recipes and
   actual inventory. Do not require the LLM to spell out navigation commands.
   Missing recipe inputs preserve the parent recipe. No reverse edge, recipe
   or successful remedy is invented to make a candidate executable.
6. Execute one action and return to step 1. A failed source changes the source
   method, not the required material. A capacity problem retains the source.
   Darkness is unknown visibility, not evidence of absent material.

Examples supported by the compiler
- Obtain a missing ingredient, then craft, then pick up the actual output.
- Acquire a publicly observed object hypothesized to enable a locked entrance.
- Inspect an accessible container; retrieve a material only after contents are
  observed. An uninformative inspection cannot establish absence or success.
- Temporarily store an unrelated carried item in an already inspected attached
  container with observed space, preserving parent ingredients and goal reserves.
These are generic operator patterns; no game, location or item name appears in
the policy. Synthetic unit coverage is distinct from native game evidence.

Revocation and cost limits
Source absence is scoped to material, quantity, location and task, expiring
after 24 actions; changed observed quantity can revoke it. In p2 an actually
failed directed entrance is deferred for at most 16 actions with the same
carried/equipped resources. A changed resource state or later successful
crossing revokes it. This restriction affects compiled repairs only. A world
can change its doors over time or by report; the finite deferral is a heuristic
and can temporarily miss such a change. It is not an infeasibility theorem.
Each plan has four dependency levels, a 64-node route search and a 16-action
window. Twelve method calls are allowed, including corrections; ordinary new
repairs stop at ten, reserving two calls for an active unresolved plan. A repeat
of the same query context is deferred for six steps. Existing valid actions
can continue after the call budget. Otherwise the ordinary actor remains the
fallback; its loops are not globally prevented.

Scope limits
Requirement extraction still uses the existing public fragment and is
incomplete. An access destination can be a prerequisite of the attempted
method without being necessary for the global task. The compiler cannot prove
the usefulness of visiting it. Candidate ranking remains LLM order after
checks, not a learned or proven estimate of task value. Unsupported dialogue,
unseen recipes, unknown reverse routes and general capacity rearrangement
remain unresolved. API-format mistakes can exhaust the single correction.
There is no guarantee of whole-game success, preservation of all future
options, optimality, or positive information value. Evaluate actual material
acquisition, task score, and action/API costs; do not substitute admission or
milestone counts for those outcomes.

Validation
See plan_protocol.txt and results/2026-10-18-local-plan-repair/RESULTS.txt.
The development study retains both frozen iterations and all losses. It does
not justify changing the default policy or claim independent generalization.
