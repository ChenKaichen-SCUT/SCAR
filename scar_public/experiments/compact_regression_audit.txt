Mark r0, checked16 -> compact15:
There is only one actual compact override, action58: enter library -> talk to
elio, triggered by repeated movement without observed progress. Both actions
succeed in same-state one-step replay; neither immediately scores. Dialogue
only repeats the copper-sword task, so its information value was not established.
It is NOT a hard prohibition of necessary transit. The other3 compact repair
queries keep the original action. Both arms first reach quest score2 at action65.
Subsequent compact progression lags: score10 at150 vs124, score11 at159 vs132,
score12 at244 vs213, score15 at296 vs256. Checked reaches16 at261; compact stops
at15 when the300-action budget expires. One altered action changes later prompts
and stochastic choices; this single paired trajectory does not prove the local
override caused every downstream delay. Failure of non-regression is observed;
systematic overblocking or stale-evidence failure is not established here.

Metropolis r1, checked10 -> compact9:
14 repair queries,12 changed actions. Two changes have only a soft repetition
trigger; both original and replacement succeed. Ten replace explicitly failed
movement; ALL ten original commands still fail in exact same-state replay.
Eight of those replacements avoid the immediate failure, while two try another
locked route and fail too. No changed action has an immediate quest/death delta.
Several replacements merely inspect objects. At actions222 and241 the same
clerks_bench is inspected; avoiding a failed door does not demonstrate task value.
In particular, the action241 midnight-related rejection does not suppress a
successful route in same-state replay: the proposed evidence_room door is locked.
This does not prove the six-step context/expiry rule handles all timed dynamics.
Compact reaches score8 at75 (checked also75), then obtains score9 from the bailiff
at267. Checked additionally gets the weapon subgoal at178 and bailiff at282.
The concrete missing stage is weapon acquisition; continuation substitutions do
not solve the upstream crafting/resource bottleneck. Candidate-menu membership
certifies access syntax only, not task progress or a valid replacement method.

Decision: freeze the existing compact policy. No game-, NPC-, item- or stage-
specific fix; no new heuristic inferred from these two outcomes. The audit
supports testing whether evidence-timed reconsideration generalizes, not a claim
that false triggers or regressive trajectories have been eliminated. Off-policy
one-step replay cannot identify the full long-horizon causal effect. Independent
new-instance evaluation and a compute-matched timing control follow this note.
