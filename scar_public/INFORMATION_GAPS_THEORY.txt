Stronger controls, cost control, and a strict structural separation
2026-10-16. The directory identifier 2026-10-17-information-gaps is a run label.

Scope and existing work
We retain finite deterministic actions, named initial states, fixed nonnegative
resource consumption, Boolean observations, unit costs, and strong finite plans.
Goal truth is not observed implicitly. An action must be legal in every current
world. All terminal states must satisfy the goal; completed worlds are retained
in real search. Only the optimistic coverage bound can absorb completed worlds.

Small-belief projections are not our invention. Rintanen (2004), Distance
Estimates for Planning in the Discrete Belief Space, formalizes n-distance
relaxations. The present pair control computes completed one/two-world finite
contingent policy costs in our semantics, not an official implementation of his
planner. It is a meaningful stronger control against a singleton maximum.
https://users.aalto.fi/~rintanj1/jussi/papers/Rintanen04heu.pdf

Labels, cross-world relationships and overlap are likewise established in
Bryce, Kambhampati and Smith, Planning Graph Heuristics for Belief Space Search
(JAIR 2006; arXiv deposit 2011). We do not claim first use of model labels,
cross-world constraints or overlapping-support reasoning.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.1711

Selective expensive heuristic evaluation has prior work in Lazy/Rational Lazy
A* and IDA*. Our fixed credit rule is a transparent cost-control ablation; it
does not reproduce their regret estimator and is not claimed as metareasoning
theory or an optimal allocation policy.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.5030
https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.6593

Sensing choices and productive plans between sensing steps also appear in
Maliah, Brafman, Karpas and Shani (ICAPS 2014), Partially Observable Online
Contingent Planning Using Landmark Heuristics. That method addresses online
planning; it is not an admissible worst-case-cost baseline for the present task.
https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICAPS/article/view/13632

Control 1: completed pair projections
For exact B and horizon H, h_pair(B,H)=max over completed subsets S of size at
most two of V_H(S), combined with the common inexpensive bound. Restricting a
strong policy for B to S yields a strong policy for S, so V_H(S)<=V_H(B).
Every maximand comes from a fully completed min/max recurrence. Interruptions
omit the unfinished maximand, not cache its partial min. Previously completed
values are still safe. The work budget is 150000 charges over the whole run;
work charged includes new transitions and recursive expansions. This capped
control may be weaker than the mathematical all-pair maximum; statistics report
fallbacks. It must not be described as exact evaluation of all pairs when capped.

Control 2: incompatible terminal histories and the observation-tree bound
Build a graph G on initial model names. Add edge (i,j) only after a synchronous
pair search proves that no common action sequence can finish both initial states.
The pair search removes all resource restrictions and ignores observation
values, but retains actual Boolean preconditions and effects (including any
effects of sensing actions). It exhausts the finite reachable pair graph. If
work is exhausted first, add NO edge. This is a generic procedure; it contains
no Doors-specific predicate or move schema checks.

Lemma. Every terminal history of a real policy contains an independent set of G.
Proof. Models that reach the same terminal history execute the same action
sequence, and both finish. That is a path in the relaxed synchronous pair graph;
therefore they cannot be a certified edge. The claim also holds after any shared
prefix, so initial conflict edges remain valid in every surviving belief. QED.

Any clique K in the induced graph on the current candidate names needs at least
|K| terminal histories. We use greedy construction of a clique, which is safe;
we do NOT misuse a greedy coloring upper bound as a lower bound. With binary
observations, depth s has at most 2^s leaves. Thus at least ceil(log2 |K|) sensing
actions are required on some branch. For resource dimension i, let m_i be the
minimum consumption by any sensing action. If m_i>0, at most floor(r_i/m_i)
observations can occur along any branch. This yields a resource infeasibility
test if |K| exceeds the permitted leaves. If no observation exists the leaf
budget is one; zero-cost dimensions impose no additional cap.

This is an elementary information-tree/control compatibility baseline, not a
newly discovered universal lower bound. A conflict-free graph can still hide
larger jointly incompatible sets, and permitted leaves can be unrealizable due
to the positions, preconditions and outcomes of actual sensing actions.

Strict separation family, coverage_separation.py
For any n>=3, the hidden initial model is i in {0,...,n-1}. All actions consume
one unit of a common resource; the initial budget is two. The goal is done.
Retained control variables are start, phase_j and end; done is not retained.
At start, prepare_j chooses an omitted model j, moves to phase_j and completes
one selected different model j+1 (mod n). At phase_j, finish_j moves to end and
completes every model except j, including the model already completed by prepare.
At end, recover completes every model. Boolean sense_i observes the hidden model
at any stage and consumes one unit. There is no uncharged sensing or stopping
observation. The horizon is three, but only two charged actions are affordable.

Proposition. Every proper subset S of model names has a <=2-action conformant
plan, but no strong policy completes all n models with this budget.
Proof. Choose an omitted j outside S, then prepare_j; finish_j. For all n,
two productive actions complete only n-1 models. A sensing-first policy has at
least one observation branch with >=2 remaining models and only one affordable
action; at start one productive action can complete only one of them. Sensing
after a preparation cannot itself complete the remaining models. Two sensing
actions do not achieve the goal. Hence no strong completion exists. Giving one
more resource unit permits prepare_j; finish_j; recover. QED.

Consequences. For every fixed k<n, the complete k-model projection is feasible
and has cost <=2. All pair terminal-compatibility graphs are empty even WITH
resources, and resource-blind recovery also makes all models compatible. The
clique/leaf control returns zero. The scalar capacity at depth two incorrectly
allows 1+(n-1)=n successes, counting the first completed model again in finish.
Candidate-identity propagation removes that name from the continuation, giving
at most 1+(n-2)=n-1. With two units it correctly proves infeasibility for H=3.

Tests exercise n=3,4,8,16 and confirm count lower=2, pair lower=2, leaf lower=0,
identity lower=infinity. Independent exact search checks n<=8 and replays the
3-resource recovery policies. This establishes strict non-dominance of these
particular controls, not novelty against every prior higher-order abstraction,
labelled planning graph, or arbitrarily large subset projection. The instances
are deliberately constructed to expose the mechanism, not public holdout data.

Cheap lower bounds and safe resource allocation
The shared signed-literal h_max is a delete relaxation: initially one rail of
each atom is available, effects may add either signed rail, and previously
available rails persist. Conjunction takes max and disjunction min; negation is
pushed through Boolean connectives. Positive oneof is relaxed to or; negated
oneof to True. This can admit inconsistent witnesses, hence is optimistic.
In a legal length-d execution, every required signed literal has relaxed level
at most d by induction on the action sequence. The fixed point's goal level is
therefore a lower bound. A partially computed fixed point is NOT used as a
lower bound: unfinished relaxation returns zero. Nominal stop thresholds are
50000 effect checks per query and 2000000 per run. The frozen implementation
tests after incrementing the counter: an interrupted query can exceed the
threshold by one, and later queries repeat that one check. Thus its reported
run counter can exceed 2000000. This is a cost defect, not a stronger bound.

Known-world BFS expands <=64 states/query, <=4096/run. It returns a shortest
policy when found, bounded-infeasibility only after exhaustion to the requested
depth, and otherwise just a completely searched layer lower bound. Search never
waits for an unbounded singleton BFS. All variants receive the same change.

Optional coverage can be interrupted only at explicit checkpoints. Completed
channel tables and DP entries are retained; unfinished min/max results are not
cached. On interruption, the planner retains the cheaper completed bound.
Unconditional coverage: nominal <=3 seconds/run, <=1 second/query.
Scheduled coverage: available credit=min(3s,0.25s+0.25*noncoverage elapsed),
<=0.25s/query, skip single-model beliefs. Exhaustion skips new calls. Deadlines
can overrun by one atomic SAT query (itself limited to 1 second), and whole
workers have a separate 15-second timeout. These are not hard real-time bounds.
Skipping an admissible estimate cannot compromise optimality of completed exact
search. It can increase search effort; no expected-time guarantee is claimed.

Independent evaluation protocol
The new source suite is fixed before inspecting planner outcomes: 26 original
public tasks from the pinned author CFF archive and pinned CPOR repository,
eight explicit sensor-budget overlays, plus five separately labelled old failure
diagnostics. Original scale sequences: Blocks 3/7/11/15, Unix 1/2/3/4, Logistics
1/3/5/7, MedPKS 10/30/50/70, Grid 2/3/4/5. Additional CPOR examples include
Doors15, Blocks2/3, Colorballs, Wumpus and noisy Localize. Syntax/ground/model
limitations are retained as exclusions, not silently replaced or removed.
https://fai.cs.uni-saarland.de/hoffmann/cff.html
https://github.com/aiplan4eu/up-cpor/tree/4776479597e33610f68dcc20613850f414eb6688/Tests

The public-instance horizon is 60 and each run uses 15 s/2 GiB; these are bounded
feasibility/optimality claims. Single deterministic timing has no significance
claim. A previously unpublished public instance is not a wholly unseen domain.
Some domains share a lineage with development domains; cohorts are explicit.
No hyperparameter changes after the holdout manifest is frozen. The synthetic
separation was developed separately and does not count toward held-out success.

Computational scope and post-holdout diagnostic
For N candidate names, masks can have 2^N distinct values. With finite Q retained
state/resource keys and horizon H, a generic relation cache can contain up to
O(|Q| H 2^N) entries, with action/channel work at each entry. We make no generic
polynomial-cost claim. In the constructed n=3,4,8,16 runs, the implementation
visited 6n+3 relation entries; this is an observed family-specific pattern.

The strict separation depends on repeated productive progress, not on a proof
that our heuristic selects better sensing actions. It is evidence of additional
joint-feasibility information, not a complete theory of optimal information
acquisition or a separation from every resource-aware labelled abstraction.

transition_budgeted_planner.py is a separately reported AFTER-holdout diagnostic.
It caps singleton BFS transition lookups at 4096/query and 65536/run, stops new
hmax allocation after the global effect counter is exhausted, and limits the
scheduled method to 32 optional calls with at least 0.025 s of available credit.
An exception inside a BFS layer retains the previously completed record only;
it does not certify that partial layer. Forced zero-budget oracle tests verify
that exact search and policy replay still agree when all BFS calls stop early.

ERRATUM to the diagnostic manifest's timing description: its nominal 1-second
allowance controls permission to continue BFS, using elapsed known-world time;
it does NOT cap all known-world work at 1 second. hmax has a separate effect-work
budget and continues after BFS is disabled; later cached calls also accumulate
in that timer. Observed totals around 3.4--3.6 s on Rovers therefore do not obey
the manifest's literal "singleton work <=1s" phrase. Treat transition counts,
the 32-call cap and the worker timeout as the enforced limits, not that phrase.
The original manifest and frozen source are retained to disclose this defect.
None of these post-holdout results replace the independent main experiment.
